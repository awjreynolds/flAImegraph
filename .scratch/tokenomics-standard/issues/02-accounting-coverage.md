# What cost and work-allocation semantics already exist?

Type: research
Label: wayfinder:research
Status: resolved
Assignee: awjre (research investigator: accounting)
Parent: ../map.md
Blocked by: none

Research branch: research/accounting-coverage

## Question

What do FOCUS and adjacent primary specifications already cover for AI usage, billed versus effective versus estimated cost, shared-cost allocation, adjustments, credits, unit comparability, reconciliation, and linking charges to tickets or epics? Can they account for per-call agent work, and which missing links require policy or richer source evidence rather than a new format? Identify released versus proposed coverage and relevant extension/contribution routes.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-06 by primary-source investigation. Released FOCUS 1.4 already defines financial cost bases, allocation conservation, invoice lineage and correction handling. BilledCost excludes estimates. FOCUS 1.5 development already covers relevant model, actor and pricing work. Candidate contribution: correspondence between execution evidence, financial records and work attribution; missing observations and allocation choices remain evidence and policy issues respectively.
