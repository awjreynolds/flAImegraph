# flAImegraph

An exploration of a common way to account for AI usage across conversations, agents, tickets, and epics, and visualise it through flame graphs.

The project is currently a planning and research effort. Potential industry adoption is a destination to investigate; no standard or implementation has been declared.

- [Research assessment and proposed route](docs/research/standards-gap-assessment.md)
- [Wayfinder decision map](.scratch/tokenomics-standard/map.md)
- [Existing implementations and proposed validation cases](docs/research/ecosystem-and-validation.md)

Primary-source research was checked on 6 September 2026. Individual reports distinguish released specifications, development conventions, open proposals, and findings that still need validation against actual exports.
