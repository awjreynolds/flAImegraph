# Profilers and capture across coding harnesses

Landscape research **7 September 2026**, supplemented with Headroom evaluation **9 September 2026** for [Compare deep profilers and native capture across other harnesses](https://github.com/awjreynolds/flAImegraph/issues/19). This is the maintained comparison; it supersedes the breadth claims of the 6 September inventory, while retaining its pinned technical audits as references.

## Decision

**There are credible direct alternatives, but none examined here is demonstrated to satisfy the whole intended workflow.** Evaluate existing capture and drilldown before selecting new implementation. The strongest complementary first validation set is **AgentSight**, **rguiu/ai-agent-profiler**, and **pi-trace-extension**, with **TokScope** as a metadata-first design and Pi-fixture comparison. This is a source-based shortlist, not a product-quality ranking or adoption decision.

TokScope is much narrower than the initial name-search impression: its implemented adapters include Pi and Copilot CLI, but **no Codex or Claude Code**. Its privacy split and honest unknowns are useful prior art; its normalized session model lacks the identities and ancestry needed for our deeper questions. The request profiler already has repeated-file-read, context and cache recommendations, so those features are not established differentiation. Neither its recommendations nor our own efficiency calculations establish equivalent accepted work at lower cost.

Keep the existing Wayfinder maps. The next capture decision is the minimum evidence needed to answer a concrete developer question; executable comparison then tests whether existing products supply it. Do not build another viewer, collector, plugin framework or exchange format solely because the first inventory missed close tools. Likewise, do not treat polished wording or a repository's existence as proof of a working alternative.

**Evidence labels:** **D** = documented by the project; **S** = static source inspected at a pin; **F** = fixture executed; **L** = live harness/UI exercised. The 7 September landscape findings are D/S, including test-code inspection. The 9 September Headroom supplement adds seven isolated component probes (**F**), with their scope explicitly bounded below. No third-party package was installed or live harness/UI tested; no private logs were read. Prior executed checks remain individually labelled in their linked reports.

## Source-to-output checks for strongest candidates

### TokScope: a real but narrower prototype

Pin [`e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e`](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/commit/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e). The [registry](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/config/load.ts) establishes actual harness support. The chain is adapter `listSessions/parseSession` → [collection pipeline](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/collect/pipeline.ts) → [SQLite upsert](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/store/sqlite.ts) → [cost projection](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/cost/project.ts) → [server](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/ui/server.ts) and [HTML](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/ui/html.ts). The read-only UI defaults to loopback and displays session totals/tier/confidence and T2 turn/tool nesting.

The [normalized schema](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/domain/types.ts) preserves unknown quantities and separates native units, but has no event-source digest/cursor, provider response ID, effort, requested/served model split or child-session links. Whole-session upsert prevents duplicate sessions on recollection; it does not reconcile duplicate native records inside one source.

The [Pi adapter](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/adapter/pi.ts) reads assistant messages as turns, pairs tool results and sums native usage. It scans bounded roots, skips subagent-artifacts and malformed lines, and does not derive retry/compaction flags. These omissions mean flag fields alone are not capability proof. The [Copilot adapter](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/src/adapter/copilot-cli.ts) uses shutdown model totals, preserves premium-request units separately, and deliberately returns totals-only T1 when per-turn usage is missing. Orphan-tool attachment to the first turn is a display convention, not proven causality. [Pi tests](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/tests/pi-adapter.test.ts) and [Copilot tests](https://github.com/dhaiiryar/tokscope/blob/e2421e7aa89a2050aa848c735327d171d53cc08e/tests/copilot-cli-adapter.test.ts) cover useful fixtures; read, not executed here.

Reusable: bounded discovery, structure-first collection, separate opt-in redacted full-text storage, fidelity tiers, native-unit separation and honest totals-only output. Neither the session JSON store nor a turn/tool list is a general forensic evidence graph.

### AI Agent Profiler: direct workflow-analysis overlap

Pin [`799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a`](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/commit/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a). [Proxy](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/proxy/proxy.ts) → [capture](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/capture/capture.ts) writes per-request NDJSON bodies and error/end records → [`runParse`](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/parse/run.ts) and [`parseTrace`](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/parse/parse.ts) → [SQLite requests/metrics/tools](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/store/store.ts) → [`exportSession/renderMarkdown`](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/cli/export.ts), including JSON export with recommendations. This is a complete statically inspected capture-to-export chain; interactive UI not exercised.

The parser normalizes OpenAI prompt tokens by subtracting cached input; context/tool estimates use characters/4. That distinction must survive export. Raw body chunks are base64, not redacted; header/URL redaction and derived-tool-argument secret scrubbing do not remove raw content. Local request IDs and idle-timeout unattributed sessions need checks for logical workflow boundaries. Export formatting maps some null usage/cost to zero, so test preservation of unknowns through the user-facing result.

[`recommend`](https://github.com/rguiu/ai-agent-profiler/blob/799213a68b6f7755d6f7b1b06884f58f962b0b9a/src/recommend/recommend.ts) implements repeated-file/tool calls, large tool-result/context patterns, cache and search-then-read advice. These are observed-pattern heuristics, not proof that the suggested change preserves correctness. Benchmark fixtures and tests exist in the repository, but their execution and claimed savings were not verified. August launcher changes adjust tool-search defaults when changing provider endpoints: a comparison must record these settings because instrumentation can change the measured workflow.

### Pi trace extension: rich local execution evidence with different defaults

Pin [`5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27`](https://github.com/npxcnency-ux/pi-trace-extension/commit/5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27). The [extension](https://github.com/npxcnency-ux/pi-trace-extension/blob/5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27/extensions/trace/index.ts) registers session/turn/message/tool/model/thinking/compaction hooks, associates tool-call IDs with steps, and writes events.jsonl. [`trace_to_html.py`](https://github.com/npxcnency-ux/pi-trace-extension/blob/5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27/extensions/trace/trace_to_html.py) reconstructs the tree; [`viewer.js`](https://github.com/npxcnency-ux/pi-trace-extension/blob/5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27/extensions/trace/viewer/viewer.js) renders usage, tool and step details. Child directories/environment and scanned child traces support nesting; this is not proof that every independent spawned session is linked.

The extension writes prompt, text, tool arguments and exposed thinking content. It records model/thinking-change events, but those are not necessarily served-effort confirmation. `step_end` keeps errors/stop reason/usage; headline interaction totals use `turn_summary`. A crash after a completed step but before its summary is therefore an explicit fixture: visible step evidence and headline totals must not be assumed equivalent. Partial fields inside present usage objects often default to zero. The failure handler disables all later recording after an error and prints stderr; it does not create a durable evidence-gap record. [Smoke-test source](https://github.com/npxcnency-ux/pi-trace-extension/blob/5441bcca041e3b3a203aee91c68e8ea809e6aa27/tests/smoke_fail_open.sh) checks normal capture and unwritable-directory host survival, not full conservation or coverage reporting. It was not run.

## Native integrations and adjacent layers

LangSmith's [Codex documentation](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/trace-with-codex) specifies Codex ≥0.128 and a concrete plugin that uploads completed transcripts with model/tool/subagent structure; accumulated inputs/outputs include content and interrupted turns flush later. Its [Pi extension](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/trace-with-pi) requires installation and explicit tracing enablement. The [coding-agent metadata contract](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/coding-agent-metadata-contract) is reusable prior art, not an independent cost-accounting standard. A configurable endpoint does not imply interchangeable backend semantics.

Langfuse's [overview](https://langfuse.com/resources/engineering/coding-agent-tracing) distinguishes hook capture, native OTLP and MCP access to already-collected traces, and acknowledges missing assembled context. Its [Pi integration](https://langfuse.com/integrations/developer-tools/pi-agent) is experimental. Earlier [Codex integration audit](langsmith-codex-reuse.md), [Gemini/OpenCode report](gemini-opencode-profilers.md) and [Pi fidelity report](pi-capture-fidelity.md) retain detailed native/adapter findings. Native VS Code inspectors, Gemini DevTools and Copilot telemetry remain valid comparison paths when those harnesses enter the selected workflow; they are not displaced by this Codex/Claude/Pi emphasis.

AgentScope is instrumentation-producer prior art, not an automatic stock-harness capture solution. Herdr contributes manager/pane/native-session attribution; [herdr-agent-quota](https://github.com/levi-qiao/herdr-agent-quota) demonstrates plugin packaging and account/quota polling, not individual inference receipts. The completed research tickets [Codex and Claude capture surfaces](https://github.com/awjreynolds/flAImegraph/issues/35), [Pi capture boundary](https://github.com/awjreynolds/flAImegraph/issues/36) and [herdr evidence informed by AgentScope](https://github.com/awjreynolds/flAImegraph/issues/37) own those details. No live interoperability was established there or here.

## Concrete validation handoff

[Validate shortlisted profilers against the representative workflow](https://github.com/awjreynolds/flAImegraph/issues/20) owns executed evidence. Start with sanitized fixtures, then a small controlled harness run and actual UI drilldown; do not install every discovered project.

1. **Common question:** within one identified work item, show which model/effort was observed, which files/tools it used, usage receipts, failed/repeated work, one child operation, completion state and acceptance evidence. Missing fields must remain visibly unknown. Ask a human whether the output answers the question.
2. **Conservation and identity:** two distinct equal-usage responses plus a duplicate copy; streaming revision; cumulative parent plus child totals; repeated import/resume. Compare against independently authored expected records. No false duplicate suppression, double-counting or invented ownership.
3. **Partial execution:** tool failure, provider error/retry when exposed, interrupted stream, truncated JSONL, completed step lacking turn summary, unwritable recorder and later resumption. Require a visible partial/gap state; no completed-looking zero totals.
4. **Context and advice:** repeated file exploration followed by acceptance checks; distinguish request-body evidence from chars/4 estimates, and suggestions from validated savings. For rguiu's proxy record endpoint/tool-search/cache settings to detect measurement-induced changes.
5. **Privacy and transfer:** verify metadata-default behavior, opt-in content, retention and source-linked export; map each candidate's JSON/pprof/event representation to our evidence model explicitly. An exporter is not plug-compatible merely because it emits JSON/OTLP.

First execute AgentSight's profile/export path, AI Agent Profiler's supported request/export path, and Pi extension's events-to-HTML path. Use TokScope's Pi fixture as a small privacy/tier comparison; add Agent-Blackbox or claude-devtools only if the first set leaves a relevant UI/graph question unresolved. Preserve the older monetary-width acceptance separately: currency rendering must not exclude otherwise useful forensic prior art.

The minimum-evidence decision and per-harness route remain open. This research resolves the landscape investigation, not the user's product choices or live-validation task. **No uniqueness, full-accounting, maintained-solution parity or proven-efficiency claim follows from this pass.**

## Headroom wrapper/proxy evaluation — 9 September 2026

**Decision: retain Headroom as a serious selective-reuse candidate; do not adopt its default wrapper unchanged as flAImegraph's recorder.** It provides real multi-provider launch/routing and request-processing seams. Our metadata-first, after-work forensic goal needs a separately verified observation configuration and a stronger evidence recorder. No implementation route or pending minimum-evidence decision is resolved by this evaluation.

Inspected source: [`headroomlabs-ai/headroom@3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16`](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/tree/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16). Apache-2.0; manifest version 0.37.0. Latest GitHub release at the check was v0.37.0, 27 August 2026, pointing to `32d7ca4577d599b8a5f811ada74cf31504302c9d`. The inspected September source is newer than that release: a package install must not be assumed to contain these fixes. Recent substantive routing, log isolation and memory failure fixes demonstrate ongoing development, not guaranteed compatibility. [Release](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/releases/tag/v0.37.0), [inspected commit](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/commit/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16).

### Wrapper and request-evidence source chains

The [wrapper](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/cli/wrap.py) starts/reuses the proxy and prepares provider-specific launch settings. Codex uses process-local base-URL/provider overrides, while surrounding setup can configure MCP/Serena and backup/recovery state. Claude routing includes settings writes and a self-healing settings hook. `--code-memory none` controls additional code-memory setup; it is not a switch for every proxy feature. This supports selective launch reuse, not an assumption of a wrapper that only sets one environment variable.

There is a dedicated [OMP adapter](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/providers/omp/runtime.py) that backs up and edits the Anthropic base URL in `models.yml`; no dedicated stock Pi wrapper was found. OMP support does not certify Pi support.

[`--no-optimize`](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/cli/proxy.py) sets `ProxyConfig.optimize=False`; `--no-cache` independently disables semantic caching. The [compression decision](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/proxy/compression_decision.py) gates compression, but [Anthropic](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/proxy/handlers/anthropic.py) and [OpenAI](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/proxy/handlers/openai.py) handlers retain processing outside that gate. Depending on other configuration, this includes memory/CCR, output shaping, turn hooks and request normalization. This is a static reason that compression-off alone cannot certify transparency; it does not mean every optional feature is enabled by default. Transport header changes must also be distinguished from semantic body changes.

For streaming, the inspected chain is route handler → [`StreamingMixin._stream_response/_finalize_stream_response`](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/proxy/handlers/streaming.py) → [`RequestOutcome.from_stream` and outcome funnel](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/headroom/proxy/outcome.py) → RequestLogger, metrics and PERF records. RequestLog has proxy request ID and optional content-derived turn ID, but no dedicated upstream response ID, physical retry identity, abort state or provider session ID. Some session/timing information is emitted separately, requiring a join; this is not a complete causal graph.

The streaming parser can extract provider usage, but missing output usage may be estimated from text or stream bytes. Its source tag and the distinction between local token counts and provider receipts must survive any adapter. The inspected `from_stream` call does not carry a separate provider-input quantity, and its constructor has no such argument. Upstream-error and generator-finally paths also invoke that constructor without an HTTP status argument while the outcome default is 200. These are **source-deduced validation risks**, not executed proxy failures: test a 529 response, a partial stream and a disconnect before trusting success counters or input billing totals. The logger's null-capable schema alone does not establish unknown preservation through the whole route.

### Validation and reuse boundary

Reuse candidates: provider-aware process launch, endpoint/auth adaptation, request/response transport, and the [differential-network capture harness](https://github.com/headroomlabs-ai/headroom/blob/3d3c629a9f349ed0f89102e32599aacea32e0b16/wiki/network-diff-capture.md). Its direct/client-side/upstream capture lanes can identify routing and body changes. The harness captures bodies, so it belongs in isolated synthetic or explicitly content-enabled testing.

Before choosing a full Headroom dependency, require the following contract on supported Codex and Claude routes:

1. Use a disposable home/workspace, dummy credentials and localhost mock upstream. Compare direct versus observation-configured paths, recording endpoint, requested model/effort, system/tool schemas, body, semantic-cache behavior and streaming result. A compression-off flag alone is not the acceptance criterion.
2. Preserve real session/response identity or explicitly declare missing identity; join native tool/lifecycle evidence separately. Verify terminal usage, retry/abort and request/response finalization without turning absent receipts into zero.
3. Disable optional content retention and all unwanted exports explicitly. Verify both storage and debug endpoints, not only JSONL output. Capture full bodies only for the synthetic wire-diff test.
4. Induce disk failure, stream disconnect, resume/replay and concurrent wrappers. Require visible coverage loss and source-linked evidence; a live savings dashboard is not sufficient.
5. Decide between embedding a small transport/launch seam and depending on the full optimizer only after those checks. Keep optimization experiments separate from baseline capture and tie any savings claim to comparable accepted work.

No wrapper, full proxy, live model, upstream service or UI was run; no harness configuration, credentials or personal transcripts were accessed. Seven isolated component probes executed successfully in the sense that their assertions reproduced the behaviors above; they are **not seven product acceptance passes**. Current stock Pi support, installed-version auth behavior and whole-proxy transparency remain unproven.
